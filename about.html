Our Family Story

17 years of love, care, and tail-wagging treats from New Jersey to Las Vegas

From New Jersey Kitchen to Las Vegas Community

In 2006, when her son James Copeland was just 13 years old, Regine Carney started baking organic dog biscuits in her New Jersey kitchen. What began as a solution to ensure her family dogs were eating wholesome, chemical-free treats has grown into a beloved brand serving dog parents across the country.

Concerned about the quality and ingredients in commercial dog treats, Regine developed a simple recipe using only human-grade ingredients: organic oats, natural peanut butter, and pure honey. No preservatives, no artificial anything - just wholesome goodness that dogs go crazy for.

In 2020, we made the exciting move from New Jersey to Las Vegas, Nevada, bringing our family business to the West Coast. Today, those homemade treats have become Foxy B biscuits. Regine serves as CEO, bringing her passion for quality and care to every batch. James, now 30, serves as CFO, handling the business operations while ensuring they never compromise on their core values.

While we've grown and relocated, our commitment remains the same: small-batch baking with the same love and attention to detail as that very first batch in New Jersey. We're proud to be a family-run business based in Las Vegas, and we're excited about our plans to open a physical storefront where customers can see our baking process firsthand.

Our Mission: Provide wholesome, organic treats made with love - honoring our New Jersey roots while baking in our Las Vegas kitchen - tailored for dog lovers who want the best for their furry family members.
